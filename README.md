The Gilded Vault — a midnight heist in three jobs. Cross the gallery, dodge the guards' vision cones and the sweeping cameras, pick the diamond's case, grab the side loot, and slip out through the skylight before the alarm finds you. Three rooms, each with its own patrols, cameras, shadows to hide in, and crates to break every line of sight. Smoke bombs buy you a few seconds of cover; sneaking buys you a little more.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Move with WASD or the arrow keys (or click to walk), hold Shift to sneak, press E to grab loot and hold it to pick the main case, Space throws a smoke bomb, Esc pauses.

The hand-in program is `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only): it copies `game/` and `screenshots/` into the output directory and writes `dossier.json` there.
